About these examples

They are dated snapshots made to show the tool, not assessments by or of the maintainers. People appear by role (maintainer A, contributor 1), the issue labels behind the themes were drafted by an LLM and not reviewed by the projects, and the plan items are suggestions. Each deck is one offline HTML file: use the arrow keys, press O for an overview, or print it to PDF.
